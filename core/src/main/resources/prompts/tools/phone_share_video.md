Share a workspace video to an app by opening that app's share sheet with the video attached (ACTION_SEND + EXTRA_STREAM). The result gives the share page; it does not send anything. Pick the recipient and tap send yourself with the phone screen tools, and read the screen afterwards to check it went out. Use a workspace video path such as /workspace/out/video/clip.mp4. If the app has no page that accepts video sharing, the result is cannot_share: use the app's own file/import control instead, or hand the step to the user.
