Sample 1-12 frames evenly from a workspace video and show them to you when the current model can see images. Use it to answer what happens in a clip, find a moment or check a scene. The result also gives duration, width/height and whether there is an audio track. Frames are samples, not the whole video; do not claim you watched everything or heard the audio. If a frame cannot be decoded, the result lists fewer frames; a video with no decodable frames is an error.
